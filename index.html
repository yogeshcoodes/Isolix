Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need an active internet connection to use Isolix?

No! Once you download the application and its bundled models, Isolix runs 100% offline on your local machine. No tracking, no uploads, absolute privacy.

What audio formats are supported for uploading?

Isolix supports all major audio formats including MP3, WAV, FLAC, AAC, OGG, and M4A. If your OS can play it, Isolix can likely split it.

How long does it take to process a song?

Processing time depends purely on your computer's CPU and RAM. On a standard modern Intel i5 processor with adequate RAM, a standard 3-minute song typically takes less than 2 minutes to separate.
